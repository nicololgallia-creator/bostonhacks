## The idea

- **One line:** A piano projected on the platform floor that you play with your feet, and the song lasts exactly as long as your wait for the train.
- **For:** people waiting on an MBTA platform, alone or next to strangers.
- **The problem, with a sourced number** (from research.md): to come from card R1.
- **Why it wins** against the judging criteria: the judge plays it with their own feet in the first 20 seconds (Presentation, Design); it runs on live MBTA data (Technical); it turns the wait, the part riders dread, into the game clock (Idea, User Fit).
- **Wow moment:** tiles slide across the floor toward your feet, you step on one and it plays the note. More people means more keys, so a stranger stepping in makes the song fuller.
- **The second layer:** the phone asks your stop, reads the live arrival time, and picks a song that ends as your train pulls in. On the train, it lines up songs until you reach your stop. (Nicolo, 11:50 AM Sat.)
- **Known prior work, said out loud:** projected floor pianos exist (MIT 6.111 projects 2006 and 2017, EyeClick sells floor games). None is tied to a train clock or built to carry onto a platform.

## Demo path

1. On the phone, pick your stop. The page shows the next trains, live, and the song that fits the wait.
2. Pick how many people are playing and tap Start.
3. Tiles slide across the floor toward the players' feet.
4. Players step on the tiles. Each hit plays its note, so the song is only heard if they play it.
5. The song ends as the train is due. The floor and the phone show the score.

## Data shapes

**Song** (`songs/<id>.json`):
```json
{ "id": "twinkle", "title": "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star", "by": "Traditional French melody, 1761", "bpm": 100,
  "notes": [ { "beat": 0, "midi": 60, "beats": 1 } ] }
```
`beat` is when the note starts, counted from 0. `midi` is the pitch (60 is middle C). `beats` is how long it lasts. Song length in seconds is `(last beat + its beats) * 60 / bpm`.

A song may also carry `"backing": [{ "beat": 0, "midi": 36, "beats": 2 }, { "beat": 0, "drum": "kick" }]`: notes the game plays by itself under the players' melody. `drum` is `"kick"`, `"snare"` or `"hat"` and has no pitch. Songs with no `backing` play the melody only.

**Feet source** (every tracker has this shape, see `src/track/mouse.js`):
`{ start(): Promise, read(): [{ u, v }] }`. Field coordinates: `u` runs 0 (left) to 1 (right) across the lanes, `v` runs 0 (far edge, where tiles appear) to 1 (the players' edge).

**Camera feet** (`src/track/camera.js`, before calibration): `[{ x, y, person }]`, `x` and `y` from 0 to 1 in the camera picture, one entry per visible foot.

**Lanes:** 1 player 4 lanes, 2 players 6, 3 or 4 players 8. Everyone shares one keyboard and one score. A note's lane comes from its pitch: the song's distinct pitches, sorted low to high, spread evenly over the lanes, low on the left.

**Play rules** (replaces the first hit rule, Sat 12:06 PM, after Nicolo's critique):
- **Square:** each lane's square is the lane's width from `v = 0.55` to `v = 1`. A tile takes 2.5 seconds to travel from `v = 0` to the hit line at `v = 0.75`.
- **Feet flicker.** The tracker drops feet for a frame or two. A foot counts as gone from a square only after no foot has been in it for 150 ms.
- **Tap tile** (a note shorter than 0.8 seconds): hit when a foot **arrives** on its square, meaning the number of feet in the square goes up, within 300 ms either side of the tile's time. Within 120 ms is `perfect`, otherwise `good`. A foot that was already standing there does not count. One arrival hits one tile, the nearest unjudged one in that lane. An arrival with no tile in reach costs nothing.
- **Hold tile** (0.8 seconds or longer): it starts when a foot arrives within the window, or is already standing on the square at the tile's time (then it is `good`). The note sounds for as long as a foot stays. Staying until 150 ms before the tail completes it. Leaving after at least half counts as `good` and keeps the combo. Leaving sooner is a miss.
- **Combo:** every hit adds one, a miss resets it to zero. Multiplier: x1 below 10, x2 from 10, x3 from 25, x4 from 50.
- **Score:** perfect 100, good 50, a completed hold adds 20 per beat held, all times the multiplier.
- **Grade at the end**, by hits over total: S from 95%, A from 85%, B from 70%, else C.
- **Engine output:** `update(nowMs, feet)` returns `{ tiles: [{ id, lane, kind: "tap" | "hold", v, vTail, state: "waiting" | "holding" | "hit" | "missed" }], events: [{ type: "hit" | "miss" | "holdStart" | "holdEnd", lane, midi, grade, u, v }], score, combo, maxCombo, multiplier, counts: { perfect, good, miss }, total, done, grade }`. `vTail` is where a hold's far end is. `events` holds only what happened this frame; `u` and `v` are where the foot was.

**Link messages** (JSON text, relayed to every other page):
```json
{ "type": "start", "songIds": ["twinkle"], "players": 2, "endsAt": 1795300000000, "stop": "Kenmore", "mode": "platform" }
{ "type": "stop" }
{ "type": "pause" }   { "type": "resume" }   { "type": "skip" }
{ "type": "calibrate" }
{ "type": "set", "volume": 0.8, "offsetMs": 120 }
{ "type": "state", "status": "idle", "songId": null, "title": null, "queue": [], "score": 0, "combo": 0, "maxCombo": 0, "multiplier": 1, "counts": { "perfect": 0, "good": 0, "miss": 0 }, "total": 0, "grade": null, "secondsLeft": 0, "camera": false, "calibrated": false }
```
`mode` is `"platform"` or `"train"`. `endsAt` is the time the train is due, in epoch milliseconds, or null. `status` is `idle`, `countdown`, `playing`, `paused`, `calibrating` or `done`. The stage sends `state` twice a second and on every change. The phone is only the remote: every command the game has is one of these messages.

Messages from a phone (every one carries `clientId` as before):
```json
{ "type": "mode", "clientId": "a1b2c3", "mode": "versus" }
{ "type": "start", "clientId": "a1b2c3", "songIds": ["twinkle"], "transit": null, "mode": "versus" }
{ "type": "skipWarmup", "clientId": "a1b2c3" }
{ "type": "set", "clientId": "a1b2c3", "warmup": false }
```
- `mode` is `"team"` or `"versus"`. `{type:"mode"}` sets the lobby's mode, only in the lobby. In any other status it is refused: "A song is running. Change the mode when it ends." A value that is not one of the two is ignored.
- `start` may carry `mode`. `"versus"` is versus, any other value is team, and no `mode` keeps what the lobby shows. A refused start changes nothing.
- `skipWarmup` ends the warm-up and begins the countdown. Sent by a phone that holds a seat, or by the stage (key W). From a phone with no seat: refused, "Take a seat to skip the warm-up." Outside the warm-up, or with no valid `clientId`: ignored, no notice. A paused warm-up cannot be skipped.
- `set` also takes `warmup: true | false` (default true). It is read at each start; changing it during a warm-up does not end that one.

State from the stage. New or changed fields only; the rest is as in "Seats and session":
```json
{ "type": "state", "status": "warmup", "mode": "versus", "winner": null,
  "warmup": { "need": 4, "beatMs": 600, "maxMs": 15000, "startedAtMs": 812340.5,
              "players": { "2": { "taps": 3, "ready": false, "tapAtMs": [812950.1, 813561.9, 814170.2] },
                           "4": { "taps": 4, "ready": true, "tapAtMs": [812941.0, 813555.0, 814160.4, 814770.3] } } },
  "layout": { "lanes": 1, "spots": [ { "id": 1, "rect": {} }, { "id": 2, "rect": {} }, { "id": 3, "rect": {} }, { "id": 4, "rect": {} } ] },
  "players": [ { "clientId": "a1b2c3", "spotId": 2, "score": 0, "bonus": 0, "bonusFor": null, "rank": 1, "...": "as before" } ],
  "settings": { "volume": 0.8, "offsetMs": 120, "warmup": true } }
```
- **`status`** is now `lobby`, `warmup`, `countdown`, `playing`, `paused` or `results`. The order is `lobby -> warmup -> countdown -> playing -> results`. `warmup` and `countdown` are both "a song has been started, play has not begun": phone screens that treat `countdown` as "starting" must treat `warmup` the same.
- **`mode`** (`"team" | "versus"`): in the lobby, the last one chosen. From the start of a song to its results, the mode that song runs as: versus with only one taken seat runs as `"team"` for that song, and the lobby goes back to showing the chosen mode afterwards. Versus needs 2 to 4 taken seats.
- **`players[].rank`**: versus only, else `null`. 1 is best, equal scores share a rank and the next rank is skipped (1, 1, 3). Live while the song plays, from `score + bonus`.
- **`winner`**: `{ spotId, score } | null`. Set only while `status` is `results` in a versus song with two or more seats left, and only when one seat is alone at rank 1. `null` for a tie, team mode, any other status, or when a seat left and fewer than two remain. `score` is that seat's song score.
- **Versus pays no bonuses.** The +50 best combo and +25 most perfects are NOT given in versus (`bonus` is 0, `bonusFor` is null), because they would break "the same top score for every player". Team mode pays them as before. `team.score` is still the sum of the seats.
- **Which seats are in the game** is `players[]` (one entry per taken seat, in seat order, in the lobby too). There is no separate `taken` field. The tracker and the floor read `players.map((p) => p.spotId)`. A song plays on the taken seats only, one lane each when 2 or more are taken (the lane rule counts taken seats, not seats in the layout).
- **Seats**: the default layout is four seats in a row (`DEFAULT_SEATS` in `layout.js`). `?seats=N` on the stage wins, then a layout saved by setup or autoplace. A saved layout that is exactly the old automatic 3 even pads is not a choice and is dropped (`keptLayout`). Any phone may claim any free seat while no song is running.
- **The warm-up** (`warmup` is `null` in every status except `warmup`, and while paused after a warm-up began). All times are the stage's clock (the same `nowMs` as `tick`), in ms.
  - It begins at a start from the lobby when `settings.warmup` is true. It is for the first song only: the next song of a queue, a skip, and a start by the very same people in the very same seats as the last warm-up (a "play again", which goes results -> lobby -> start) go straight to the countdown. A warm-up that was stopped before it ended does not count, so the next start warms up again. A warm-up that timed out or was skipped counts.
  - `need` 4 taps per taken seat. `beatMs` 600: the floor pulses a target every 600 ms counted from `startedAtMs`; the taps do not have to be on the beat. `maxMs` 15000: the warm-up ends 15 s after `startedAtMs` whatever happened. `startedAtMs` moves later by the time spent paused.
  - A tap is an arrival: the number of feet standing in the seat's pad rises. A foot with `lifted: true` does not stand. A foot gone for under 150 ms (the engine's flicker rule) has not left. Two feet landing together are one tap. A foot already standing when the warm-up begins is not a tap. `players["<spotId>"]` has `taps`, `ready` (`taps >= need`) and `tapAtMs` (each tap's time, at most 32). Keys are the seat ids as strings.
  - It ends the moment every taken seat is `ready`, at `maxMs`, or at `skipWarmup`. The 3 s countdown starts at that tick. A seat that is not ready still plays. At `maxMs` only, the phones of the seats not ready get `notice`: `{ for: <clientId>, text: "The floor did not see your taps. Lift your foot higher." }`. With more than one such phone the single notice slot is for everyone (`for: null`). A skip gives no notice.
  - `pause`, `resume`, `stop`, `skip` and a phone going quiet behave as in the countdown. A quiet phone keeps its seats. Pause freezes the warm-up clock; resume carries on. `skip` during a warm-up goes to the next queued song's countdown, or the lobby.
  - What the floor reads during `warmup`: `tick()` returns `{ status: "warmup", pads: [{ spotId, lanes, tiles: [] }] for each taken seat, songMs: null, song: <the song loaded> }`. `snapshot().song` is `{ id, title, seconds }`, `queue` is the rest, `players[].score` etc. are zero, `team.total` is the sum of the parts. `secondsLeft` is the song's length plus the 3 s countdown and 2.5 s slide-in, held still (it does not count down). The pads exist so the seated tracker can keep giving feet per lane. `songMs` is `null` in the warm-up and in a pause that began there.
  - Stage events from `drain()`: `{ type: "status", status: "warmup" }` on entry, and `{ type: "warmupTap", spotId, taps, atMs }` on every tap (for a click sound and a flash).
  - The stage's `?demo=1` Space (and `autostart=1`) sends `set warmup:false` first, so the demo goes straight to the countdown.

**Layout** (saved on the stage in localStorage as `pp:layout`):
```json
{ "posture": "seated", "lanes": 3, "spots": [ { "id": 1, "rect": { "u": 0.04, "v": 0.1, "w": 0.28, "h": 0.86 } } ] }
```
A spot is one seat. `rect` is its pad on the field, in field coordinates. `posture` is `"seated"` (3 lanes a pad) or `"standing"` (4 lanes a pad). One to four spots, side by side. Inside a pad, pad coordinates work like field coordinates: `u` 0 to 1 across its lanes, `v` 0 (far edge) to 1 (the player's edge), and the Play rules apply inside each pad. A foot belongs to a spot when it is inside the pad or within a tenth of the pad's size outside it. A note's lane comes from its pitch as before, spread over the pad's lanes. Every seat gets the same song on its own pad. One engine runs for each taken seat: `createGame({ song, lanes })`.

`transit` may be null. `mode` is `"platform"` or `"train"`. `endsAt` is when the train is due or the ride ends, in epoch milliseconds.

```json
{ "type": "state", "status": "lobby", "notice": null,
```

`status` is `lobby`, `countdown` (3 seconds), `playing`, `paused` or `results` (8 seconds, then the next song in `queue`, or back to `lobby`). `song` is `{ "id", "title", "seconds" }` while one is loaded. `spotId` is null until the player picks a seat. `notice` is one plain sentence when a command was refused ("That seat is taken.", "Pick a seat before starting."), cleared by the next accepted command. - **One slice a seat.** The camera picture is cut into one upright slice for each seat. The pose model runs on each slice by itself with one person to find, which is the case that works. A slice's feet belong to that seat.
- **Which lane:** a seat's two feet are its two lanes: the foot further left in the picture is one lane and the other foot is the other, with a "swap" switch for each seat in case the pad is mirrored.
- **Tap in place:** a foot's height in the picture is watched. Resting height is the ground. A foot that rises above it and comes back down has tapped, at the moment it lands. A foot that stays down is holding.

## Wave 7 (Sat 5:25 PM): fewer lanes

Nicolo, 5:25 PM: "1 player gets two note lanes (one per foot), 2 players get 2 note lanes (one note per person), 3 players get 3 lanes (one note per person), 4 players get 4 lanes (one per person) that way we dont have to cut the song up amongst 8 notes and have it get boring for people." This **replaces** "every pad has 2 lanes" in Wave 3b.

- **Lanes in a pad:** 2 when exactly one seat is playing, 1 when two or more are. So the song is spread over 2, 2, 3 or 4 lanes in all.
- **`splitParts(song, seats)`:** one seat gets the whole song over 2 lanes, as now. Two, three or four seats get the balanced keyboard over that many lanes, one lane a seat: each seat's notes all carry `lane: 0`.
- **The State's `layout.lanes`** is the lanes in a pad for the seats taken right now: 2 with zero or one seat taken, 1 with two or more. It is fixed for a song when the song starts.
- **Feet to lanes:** in a 2-lane pad foot 0 is lane 0 and foot 1 is lane 1. In a 1-lane pad both feet are on the one lane, and either foot landing is a tap.
