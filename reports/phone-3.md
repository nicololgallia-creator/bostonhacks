# phone-3, card P3 (the remote while a song plays, the results, and settings)

Sat 21 Nov 2026. Matched `shots/D1-390-3-seated.png` and `shots/phone-2-4-set-platform.png` with tokens and `DESIGN.md` only: no new colour, size, radius or motion. I had no skill tool, so I followed `DESIGN.md`, `reports/designer.md` and the short version of `notes/My design taste.md` by hand.

## Done
- `src/phone/play.js` and `play.css` (new): the live screen (countdown, playing, paused) and the results. Live: song title, one line saying what the floor is doing, the clock ("Train in 2 min 9 s" when the State has `transit`, else "Song ends in 20 s"), your score and combo with the multiplier in your seat colour, then Pause or Resume, Skip, Stop. Stop needs a second tap within 3 seconds: the button turns into "Tap again to stop", takes the whole row and Skip hides, then it reverts. No seat: the same screen, no score, "You are watching this song. Pick a seat when it ends." Results: grade large in the seat colour, score, best combo, perfect, good and miss, every seat ranked with names (your row ringed), and one line for what is next ("Next song in 5 s: Ode to Joy", or "That was the last song. Back to the lobby in 5 s."). It reuses `.row`, `.order` and `.foot` from `ride.css`.
- `src/phone/settings.js` and `settings.css` (new): a sliders button in the top bar (lobby, joined, nothing else open) opens one screen with Back: Sitting or Standing, 1 to 4 seats (`layout`, with the line about players moving out of seats that go away), volume minus and plus in steps of 10 percent (`set`), "Line up the camera" (`calibrate`, then "Setup is open on the laptop."), lag minus and plus in steps of 10 ms from 0 to 400 (`set`) with "The lag test in setup measures this for you." It closes by itself when a song starts.
- `src/phone/main.js` (small edits): imports; `settings` and `play`; `render()` hands the screen to `play` whenever the State is not `lobby` and the player has joined; the gear button; `ready` added to the redraw key; on the seated screen in the lobby, when `ready.camera` or `ready.calibrated` is false, "The floor is not lined up yet. Run setup on the laptop." under "Pick the music".
- `src/phone/mock.js` (rewritten, same shape): a whole song. Countdown, playing with scores that change every half second, pause and resume, results, the queue's next song, back to the lobby. Accepts `start`, `pause`, `resume`, `skip`, `stop`, `layout`, `set`, `calibrate`, and refuses with the real session's sentences. Test hooks in the address: `&crowd=1` (Ana in seat 1), `&seat=3` (seats whoever joins), `&running=playing|paused|results` (a song already going, started by Zo), `&transit=platform|train`.

## Calls I made
- Settings opens from a button in the top bar, not from a third button on the seated screen: the free space under the pads has no room left at 667px high, and the top bar has none used.
- Volume and lag are minus and plus steps, not a slider: one control for both, big targets, no dragging against the State arriving twice a second. A tap is shown at once and the stage's older answer is ignored for a second.
- On a train ride the clock reads "<stop> in 2 min 8 s" (where you get off), on the platform "Train in 2 min 9 s". It counts from `transit.endsAt` on the phone's own clock, so a phone with a wrong clock is out by that much.
- Skip has no second tap; only Stop does, as the card says.
- Seat colour appears only on the player's own score, combo, grade and ranking circles, each ranking circle in its own seat's colour.
- Results stay on screen only as long as the stage keeps `results` (8 s). There is no way to keep them open.
