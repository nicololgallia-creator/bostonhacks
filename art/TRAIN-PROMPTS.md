## How to make the other four

In the image tool, attach `green.png` as the style reference if the tool allows it, and say "same art style as the attached picture". Then paste one prompt. Ask for a wide picture, about 3 to 2.

The colours and shapes below are from memory of the real trains, not checked against photos today. Put a photo of the real train next to the result and fix the prompt if it is off.

**The prompt that made the Green Line train:**

> Adorable hand-drawn cartoon train with a happy face, modeled off of the actual MBTA Green Line trolley in Boston: green body, white roof, two short cars joined in the middle, a pantograph. Side view facing right. Chubby rounded shape, the headlights are big shiny eyes and the bumper is a little smile, rosy cheeks. Thick dark brown outline, flat warm colours, simple soft shading. Plain white background, no text, no logos, no scenery.
