# Demo video script

Draft 1, Sat 21 Nov. Not approved by Nicolo or Zohayb yet, not recorded.

The rule: Devpost needs a **1-minute video demo**, due 9:00 AM Sunday.

## The model: Lucid, HackPrinceton's overall winner

Zohayb asked for Lucid's structure (youtube.com/watch?v=IY0Rr7l58Eg, 1:40). It runs in six beats:

1. **The number** (0:00). Black words on a white screen, one sentence at a time: "Every year, 69 million people suffer a head injury."
2. **Why us, then the name** (0:07). A teammate's grandfather, then "So, we built Lucid".
3. **How it works, with a comparison** (0:14). "Think of a metal detector." Two clean diagrams.
4. **The hardware** (0:31). Photos of the real device, wires showing, with hand-drawn circles and arrows.
5. **The software** (0:57). A screen recording, zoomed in on one moment at a time.
6. **The close** (1:26). The name again and one sentence that says what changes.

No faces and no talking to camera. One voice over photos, diagrams and a screen recording.

Three other winners were checked. Lincoln (1:54) has the same shape: numbers first, "so we built" within 25 seconds, the hardware labelled, the agent last. Pear (1:51) and Project Xavier (0:45) skip the opening number and the closing line and read as walkthroughs.

## The script (60 seconds, about 140 words spoken)

Lucid's shape, cut from 100 seconds to 60. Voice-over throughout.
